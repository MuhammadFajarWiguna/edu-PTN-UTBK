═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  🐛 DEBUG COMMANDS (Browser Console - F12)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Check Session:
  console.log(localStorage.getItem("utbk_token"));
  console.log(localStorage.getItem("utbk_user"));

Check All Users:
  console.log(JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem("utbk_registered_users")));

Check Passwords:
  console.log(JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem("utbk_dummy_passwords")));

Force Logout:
  localStorage.removeItem("utbk_token");
  localStorage.removeItem("utbk_user");
  location.reload();

Clear All Data:
  localStorage.clear();
  location.reload();
